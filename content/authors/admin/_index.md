### About me

I am a Research Assistant at the Power Electronics and Machines Centre (PEMC), University of Nottingham. I develop electrical machines for electrified transport, combining **multiphysics modelling, experimental validation and physics-informed AI**.

I received my Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from Southeast University in 2025, following my M.Eng. (2021) and B.Eng. (2018). My doctoral research brought hairpin-winding traction machines from design through manufacture to dynamometer testing, in collaboration with Inovance.
