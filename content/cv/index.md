## Research

Electrical machines for electrified transport; electromagnetic, thermal and structural modelling; physics-informed AI; digital twins; high-speed traction machine design and experimental validation.

## Appointment

**2025–present · University of Nottingham**  
Research Assistant, Power Electronics and Machines Centre (PEMC). Research in the EPSRC AIMD programme on magnet-free synchronous machines, an in-house Python finite element package, and neural surrogates for machine design.

## Education

- **2018–2021 · M.Eng., Electrical Engineering, Southeast University.** Thesis: *Analysis and Design of High-Performance Wound-Excited Magnetic Field Modulation-based Brushless Machines*.
- **2014–2018 · B.Eng., Electrical Engineering, Southeast University.**

## Research and engineering achievements

- **23 journal papers**, including 19 in IEEE Transactions and 11 as first author; **18 conference papers** and **9 patents**. [Full publication list](/publications/).
- Technical lead for two high-speed EV traction machines developed with **Inovance**, from specification and drawing release through manufacture to AVL dynamometer validation.
- **20,000 rpm traction machine:** measured 270 kW, 380 Nm, 7.87 kW/kg and 97.4% peak efficiency.
- Developed an **in-house Python finite element package** for electromagnetic, structural-modal and mechanical-stress analysis.
- Developed a magnetic field surrogate achieving **more than 94% less computation time than FEA**, with R² = 0.9996 for torque and 0.9730 for iron loss on unseen designs.
- Developed thermal networks validated against thermocouple measurements and rotor models checked against a **22,500 rpm overspeed test**.
